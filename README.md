# Monthly Operations Review

This project reviews the financial performance and customer experience of three store branches. The analysis was completed in Excel and includes profitability calculations, a net profit comparison chart, customer feedback rankings, management recommendations, and a cost-reduction scenario for Branch C.

## Contents

The Excel workbook includes:

- Net profit calculations for Branch A, Branch B, and Branch C
- Profit margin calculations
- A column chart comparing net profit across the three branches
- Customer experience rankings based on customer feedback
- A recommendation for the Performance Bonus
- A recommendation for the Process Improvement Plan
- A bonus calculation showing the effect of reducing Branch C's operating costs by 15%

## Key Findings

Branch B achieved the strongest overall performance, with the highest net profit of $17,500 and the highest profit margin of 45.45%. It also received the best customer feedback because customers praised its product availability and friendly staff.

Branch A performed reasonably well, earning a net profit of $13,000 and a profit margin of 28.89%. Customers appreciated its service, although long queues affected the overall experience.

Branch C generated the highest sales but had the lowest profitability because of its high operating costs. It earned a net profit of only $4,000 with a profit margin of 7.69%, while customers also reported expensive shipping and slow delivery.

## Management Recommendation

Branch B should receive the Performance Bonus because it produced the best financial results while also delivering the strongest customer experience. Branch C should be placed on a Process Improvement Plan to reduce operating costs and address problems with shipping costs and delivery speed.

## Bonus Challenge

If Branch C reduces its operating costs by 15%, its operating cost would decrease from $48,000 to $40,800. Its new net profit would be $11,200, and its new profit margin would improve to 21.54%.
